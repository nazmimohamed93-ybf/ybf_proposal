Executive Summary

A Vision for Independent Minds

YBF is an education-focused NGO designed to bridge the widening gap between traditional schooling and the rapidly evolving AI-driven economy. We move beyond theory, embedding real-world entrepreneurship into the school ecosystem.

Our Vision

Students as creators, builders, and entrepreneurs instead of job dependents.

Our Mission

Equip students with AI literacy, business execution skills, and financial independence.

"We are not teaching students about business. We are building Malaysia’s next generation of entrepreneurs."

"Build early exposure to business and creativity through playful execution."

  • Basic Selling Skills
  • Creativity & Confidence Workshops
  • The "RM50 Challenge" Projects

Learning Outcome

Students gain confidence in value exchange and move from passive consumers to active initiators. They understand that money is a result of value creation.

Niaga Starter

Form 2 (Age 14)

"Introduce structured business systems and teamwork in a digital-first world."

  • Team Roles (CEO, CMO, CFO)
  • Digital Tools (Canva, TikTok Marketing)

System Exposure
